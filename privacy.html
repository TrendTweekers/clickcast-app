Privacy Policy

Clickcast for Mac · Last updated 23 October 2026

Short version: Clickcast never records what you type. It collects no data, has no account, no analytics and no tracking, and makes no network connections of its own.

Your keystrokes

With your permission (Input Monitoring), Clickcast is told when a key goes down so it can show that key on your screen for about a second. It then forgets it. Clickcast never stores, logs or sends the keys you press or the text you type.

Your clicks and pointer

Clickcast reads where your pointer is and when you click, only to draw the highlight and the click rings on your screen. Nothing is stored or sent.

What Clickcast stores

Your settings, kept on your Mac: colours, sizes, the keystroke style and position, and which features are on. Nothing else.

Purchases

Clickcast Pro is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the payment. The developer receives no payment details or personal information from Apple.

Children

Clickcast collects no data from anyone, including children.
